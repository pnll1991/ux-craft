01 / Decision example

Principles are useful only when they change a real decision.

Before

“Apply Hick's law and make the page simpler.”

No observed problem. No decision criteria. No acceptance check.

After

Observed: plans are visually equal and users must open each one to compare scope.

Change: expose differentiating attributes in one comparison, keep every legitimate plan available, and test whether a suitable plan can be chosen without opening multiple pages.
