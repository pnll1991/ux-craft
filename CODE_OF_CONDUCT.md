# Community conduct

Discuss the work with respect. Critique ideas, evidence and implementation—not a contributor’s identity or worth. Participation is welcome across backgrounds, skill levels and languages.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, doxxing and sharing private information are not acceptable. Do not pressure contributors into unpaid commitments or contact them outside channels they have offered.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close unproductive discussions or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and based on behavior. Report concerns without republishing sensitive information; use an available private channel when one has been provided.
